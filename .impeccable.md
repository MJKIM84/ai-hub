## Design Context

### Users
The creator uses Robot Lab for robotics research, portfolio demonstrations and hands-on simulation. Visitors inspect robot roles, maps, people, cargo and the actual sequence of work. These uses are stated in the project conversation.

### Brand Personality
Clear, practical and approachable. The creator requests plain Korean, visible controls, larger maps and fewer unnecessary explanatory blocks.

### Aesthetic Direction
For robot appearance, the creator explicitly references the existing Spot model. Use recognisable industrial bodies, wheels and articulated arms with restrained material detail. Preserve the existing bright map and recently generated industrial surface materials. These are authored research robot designs, not manufacturer replicas.

### Design Principles
- Make robot roles distinguishable by form and details as well as colour.
- Keep real geometry, motion, cargo and observations authoritative; decoration cannot imply a new capability.
- Preserve selection, camera tracking and physical diagnostic views.
- Make maps and robot actions the primary visual content.
- Do not redesign unrelated screens for a focused visual request.
